## Diagnosis

Dr. Matías Crespo had a name and the will to build a practice. No brand, no logo, no website, no content, no structure. In a trust-sensitive field like healthcare, that gap is the real problem: patients decide who to trust before they ever book. [SUMAR: qué tenía que lograr el sitio para ser rentable, ej. que lo encuentren, que reserven.]

![Before: nothing](./01-diagnosis.webp)

## Decision

Start from the patient's decision, not from the logo. I defined the information architecture first: what a new patient needs to see, in what order, to feel safe enough to get in touch. Only then did the visual identity follow. [SUMAR: la decisión concreta más fuerte, ej. qué dejaste afuera, cómo ordenaste los servicios, por qué esa tipografía y color.]

![Information architecture and brand system](./02-decision.webp)

## Result

A complete system built from zero: logo, brand manual, typography, color palette and a live website on WordPress/Divi, all delivered end to end by one person. [SUMAR: número o hecho, ej. consultas por mes, tiempo de entrega, comentario del cliente.]
